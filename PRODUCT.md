# PRODUCT.md — Best Management & Consulting (BMC)

register: brand

## Product purpose

BMC is a management consulting cabinet based in Ariana, Tunis, with more than fifteen
years of practice. It advises companies, investors and public institutions on strategy,
corporate finance, investment structuring, upgrade programmes (*mise à niveau*) and
organisational transformation.

The website is the cabinet's credibility surface. Most of its business arrives by
referral, so the site is rarely the first touch. It is the second one: the place a
prospect goes after a name has been mentioned to them, to decide whether these people are
serious. Its single job is to make a director, a banker or an investor think *this is a
real cabinet, and the work will hold up in front of a credit committee.*

## Strategic positioning

**Transformer la stratégie en résultats.**

BMC does not sell frameworks. It sells defensible decisions: a business plan a bank will
actually finance, a feasibility study that survives due diligence, a restructuring that
holds. The differentiator is rigour and follow-through. The cabinet stays through
implementation and measures the outcome, rather than delivering a document and leaving.

The central creative concept is **the ascent**: a trajectory that is plotted, argued and
then actually climbed. It comes from the client's own logo, which contains a rising arrow
breaking out of a frame.

## Users

- **Primary:** owners and directors of Tunisian SMEs and mid-caps (industry, aeronautics,
  automotive, agrifood, energy, health, logistics) who need financing, a formal plan, or
  a turnaround, and who will be judged by a bank or a state programme on the quality of
  the file.
- **Secondary:** investors and funds assessing a Tunisian or North African opportunity,
  who need an independent local read.
- **Tertiary:** public institutions and donor programmes commissioning evaluations,
  governance work, or upgrade schemes.

All three arrive sceptical of consultants. None of them are impressed by adjectives. They
are convinced by evidence of method, precision of language, and discretion.

## Brand voice

Three physical-object words: **bound, exact, discreet.**
Like a printed information memorandum: numbered exhibits, tabular figures, a wine seal on
the cover, nothing decorative that is not also structural.

French is the primary language and the register is professional French, not translated
English. Sentences are short and declarative. The cabinet would rather state one
verifiable thing than five impressive ones.

Voice anchors:

- "Produire des décisions défendables."
- "Le résultat se constate, il ne se déclare pas."
- "Comprendre avant de proposer."
- "Des recommandations exécutables avec vos moyens et vos équipes."

## Anti-references (what this must never look like)

- Generic AI landing page: centred hero, three icon cards, purple gradient, glass blur.
- Generic consulting site: navy and gold, stock photo of a handshake, a skyline, or four
  people pointing at a laptop.
- Editorial-magazine cosplay: drop caps, italic pull quotes, broadsheet columns. This is a
  memorandum, not a Sunday supplement.
- Clevertec Engineering. Different client, different register. No dark graphite canvas, no
  amber signal, no monospace spec labels, no WebGL particle field, no "complexity becomes
  clarity" thesis. Shared engineering standards only, never shared appearance.

## Banned copy

révolutionner · solutions clés en main · synergies · disruption · sur-mesure et
innovant · accompagnement 360° · leader du marché · partenaire de confiance ·
next-generation · unlock · empower · seamless · game-changing.

No fabricated metrics, no invented client names, no logo wall of companies BMC has not
worked with. The only figures on the site are ones the brief actually supports: fifteen
years, eight areas of expertise, ten sectors, five phases.

No em dashes anywhere in copy, in any language.

## Aesthetic lane

**The bound information memorandum.** Warm ivory stock, deep pine ink, a bordeaux seal.
Numbered chapters, running heads, hairline rules, tabular figures, plates that break to
full-bleed pine. The one moving idea is a performance curve that is drawn as you read and
that carries the reader's position through the document.

Palette is derived from the client's existing logo (teal `#1a9a9d`, wine `#9e4065`) and
matured rather than replaced, so the cabinet keeps its identity and loses the 1990s
clip-art execution.
